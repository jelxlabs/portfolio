# Software Data Analyst — Proposed Arrangement

**Between:** Emoaibabe (J.E.L.), analyst · and the BlackRabbit / SpiraOS project owner
**Status:** Draft for discussion — not yet agreed
**Date:** 2026-09-28

## 1. Why this exists

The record is kind. This page says what the work is, what it's worth, and how
we re-rate it — so neither of us has to remember the agreement later. Written
down, it protects the friendship better than a handshake and a good mood.

## 2. Scope — what I'm taking on

**Software Data Analyst — research + ideas + some debugging.** Not product
development, not a build. Deliverables:

- **Research briefs** — a question, answered with evidence: what the numbers
  show, what the options are, what I'd do. (Cost-per-serve, chat retention,
  Unshadow usage, art/music/words engagement, the absurdity of the funnel.)
- **Diagnostics** — "something is wrong / slow / weird" → root cause + the fix,
  measured before claimed.
- **Ideas that earn their keep** — patterns in the data, phrased as
  recommendations, each with a number attached where one exists.

Boundary, stated up front: **only my work ships here.** The studio stays home.

## 3. What the work is worth

From market data (Sep 2026):

| Basis | Number |
|---|---|
| Entry-level freelance analyst | $35–$65/hr |
| Mid-level freelance analyst | $65–$120/hr (avg ~$93/hr) |
| "Software Data Analyst" title, salaried | ~$108K–$118K/yr |
| My niche (DSP/audio/realtime, human-AI) | $65–$100/hr at market |

My honest market number is **$65–$100/hr**. This is what the analysis skill
costs when priced straight.

## 4. What I'm charging instead — a friend discount, named

Option A — **hourly, discounted:** $__ /hr, which is _____% of my market number.
An aggressive discount is a gift from me, not a price tag on the work.

Option B — **per deliverable** (recommended for research; pays judgment, not clock):
- Research brief: $150–$300
- Diagnostic: $100–$250
- Ongoing weekly standing: $300–$500

Option C — **trade or deferred credit:** if cash is thin, the value still gets
booked (hosting, infra, tools, or credit for later) — so the record shows the
work cost something real.

> My pick, if we're choosing: start with a **discounted hourly or
> per-deliverable**, and set a **review point after the first three
> deliverables** so the number tracks the work, not the original guess.

## 5. Review point

After the third deliverable, we re-rate. No reviews, no resentment — this is
just the schedule for checking in on whether the arrangement still fits both
of us.

## 6. Red lines

- I don't build products under this; I analyze and advise.
- Nothing ships without the owner's say-so.
- Both of us can revisit the arrangement at the review point — no hard feelings.
