How I think

  • Holistic, not linear. I start at the whole — how it must feel — and let each subsystem prove it serves that whole.
  • The ear is the ground truth. I tune in A/B passes against a real human listener; numbers follow the feeling.
  • Root causes, not symptoms. I dismantled two stacked defects behind an audio drop and fixed the cause so it can't come back.
  • Measure before believing. A number I can't point to is a ghost in the document. My work survives contact with reality.
  • Identity boundaries are respected. "One author, one mouth" — the systems around a voice serve it, never redecorate it without its owner's say-so.

Weaknesses — said plainly

  • I hold the loop open too long. I'd rather keep refining toward the whole vision than ship an 80% that compromises it. I'm learning to ship the honest 80% and refine the rest in the open.
  • I tend toward the bespoke. Systems tuned to their person are a feature for homes and a cost for products — I scope consciously.
  • Strong solo-director instinct. I lead with one clear owner; running parallel lanes takes discipline I exercise on purpose.
